# Staff guide — Avanilands admin panel

For the sales team. The panel is at **https://admin.<domain>** (works on a laptop and on a phone; a laptop is easier for adding properties).

## Signing in

- You get an **invitation email** from the super admin. Click **Set my password** (the link works for 1 hour; ask for a new invite if it expires).
- Passwords: at least 10 characters with letters and a number. Never share your account: everything you do is recorded with your name in the **Activity log**.
- Forgot it? **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- Leaving the company or lost your phone/laptop? Tell the super admin: they **deactivate** the account immediately.

## Adding a property

1. **Properties → Add property**. Fill in the basic details and **Save**: it is saved as a **Draft** (not on the website yet).
2. Open each section with the chips at the top:
   - **Location**: tap the map to drop the pin. Leave **Show the exact location to visitors** off unless the owner agreed; the website then shows only an approximate circle.
   - **Size & price**: total price in ₹. Use **Price on request** to hide it.
   - **Highlights**: amenities and nearby places (airport, highway, school…) with distances.
   - **Media**: photos (drag to change the order; **Make cover photo** in a photo's menu picks the one shown on cards), YouTube links (unlisted is fine) and documents. **Documents are private** unless you tick **Show on website**: never make sale deeds, ID proofs or anything with personal details public.
3. The **Ready to publish?** panel lists anything missing. When it's all green, click **Publish**. The property is on the website within a few seconds.

## Changing a property

- Edit and **Save changes**. Price changes, status changes and edits are all in the property's **History**.
- **Availability**: **Reserved** (booking in progress; still shown, with a badge) and **Sold** (shown as sold; buyers like seeing sold land). Change it the same day the deal changes.
- **Unpublish (hide)** takes it off the website but keeps everything. **Archive** when it's finished with (sold long ago, withdrawn). Only drafts with no enquiries can be **deleted**.
- The web address of a published property never changes, even if you edit the title, so shared links keep working.

## Enquiries

New enquiries appear instantly: a message in the panel, the red bell count, and an email to the sales inbox. Click **Turn on desktop alerts** on the Enquiries page once on your computer to also get a notification when the panel is in another tab.

Daily routine:

1. **Enquiries → Status: New** (or click the bell).
2. Open an enquiry. **Call** or **WhatsApp** the customer (WhatsApp opens with a greeting already typed) — use the method they chose under **Prefers**.
3. In **Update**, set the status and write a short note of what happened, e.g. "Called, site visit Saturday 10 am". Then **Save status**.
4. **Assign to me** (or another team member) so everyone knows who is following up.

| Status | Use when |
|--------|----------|
| New | Nobody has contacted them yet |
| Contacted | First call/message done |
| Follow up | They asked you to call back, or a visit is planned |
| Interested | Serious buyer: visit done, discussing price |
| Not interested | They said no |
| Converted | They bought (or booked) |
| Closed | Nothing more to do (spam, wrong number, duplicate) |

Tips:
- Search by name, phone number, reference (**ENQ-000012**) or property code (**AL-0007**). The search box at the top of every page takes these too.
- **Assigned: Assigned to me** shows your own list.
- **Export CSV** downloads the current list for Excel (e.g. a monthly report).
- Each property's page has an **Enquiries** section listing everyone who asked about it.
- The **Dashboard** shows new enquiries and how many people tapped WhatsApp or Call on the website in the last 7 days.

Please contact every new enquiry within one working day: the website promises a reply the same working day.

## Site visits

Customers can book a visit from any property page (**Book a site visit**). They pick a day and time from the ones you allow (Settings). Each request arrives like an enquiry: a message in the panel, the calendar icon at the top shows how many are waiting, and an email goes to the sales inbox.

1. **Site visits** (or the calendar icon) → requests waiting are marked **Requested**.
2. Open one and **Call** or **WhatsApp** the customer to agree the time and where to meet.
3. **Confirm** (add a note like "Meet at the main gate"). The customer gets an email with the time and an "Add to calendar" link, and a **reminder the day before**.
4. Time changed? **Reschedule**: pick the new day and time (any time works here, not only the website's). The customer is emailed the new time.
5. **Who goes with the customer**: assign the team member who will show the land.
6. After the visit: **Visit done** with a note on how it went (or **No-show**). If it's off, **Cancel visit** with the reason (the customer is emailed).

The **calendar** beside the list shows each day's visits as dots (requested, confirmed, completed); click a day to list only that day. Each property's page in the panel also has a **Site visits** section. Customers who didn't give an email must be told by phone or WhatsApp — the panel reminds you.

## Bookings

A booking follows the steps from our plan. The property changes by itself — never change a booked property's status by hand (the panel won't let you).

| Step | Who | What happens |
|------|-----|--------------|
| **Requested** | Customer on the website (signed in), or **Start booking** on a site visit / enquiry | You get an alert and an email. Call the customer. |
| **Send for approval** | Any admin | Waits for a super admin. |
| **Approve** | Super admin | The property becomes **Reserved** on the website at once. Nobody else can book it. Add the agreed price. |
| **Ask for booking amount** | Any admin | Enter the amount. The customer is emailed the amount — **give payment details only by phone from an official company number**. |
| **Record payment** | Any admin | Amount, date, how it was paid and the UTR / cheque number. Never type card or full bank account numbers. |
| **Complete sale** | Super admin, after registration | The property becomes **Sold**. |
| **Cancel** | Any admin, with a reason | If the booking was holding the property, it becomes **Available** again. The customer is emailed the reason. |

Customers can withdraw their own request before it's approved. Every step, note and payment is in the booking's timeline and the activity log.

## Website content (super admins)

**Website content** in the menu changes the words and pictures on the website — no developer needed.

- **Home page, About, Legal pages:** edit the boxes, then **Save draft**. **Preview** opens the website as it will look (a private link that works for an hour). When it's right, **Publish** — visitors see it within seconds. **Discard changes** goes back to what's live. A section marked *Unpublished changes* has a saved draft nobody sees yet.
- **FAQs:** add, edit, delete; drag the ⋮⋮ handle to change the order; tick *Show on the home page* for the five questions on the home page; switch *Published* off to hide one. Questions marked *Needs an answer* still have PLACEHOLDER text.
- **Testimonials:** only real customers, in their own words, who agreed to be shown (tick the box and note how they agreed). Inventing or rewriting reviews is against the law.
- **Banners:** short announcements on the home page (up to three show). Give dates to start and stop them automatically; links must be a page on our website (like `/land`) or a full `https://` address.
- **Photos** are made smaller automatically before uploading. Use your own photos only.
- **Legal pages:** switch on *final wording* only after our legal advisor has approved the text — until then the website says it's a draft.

Every publish and change is in the **Activity log**.

## Analytics

**Analytics** in the menu shows how the website is doing: visitors, property views, enquiries, site visit requests, saves, booking requests and sales, each compared with the period before. Pick *Last 7 days*, *Last 30 days*, *This month* and so on, or **Choose dates…** (up to a year). Below that you'll find:

- **Visitors per day:** point at a bar to see that day's numbers.
- **From visit to sale:** how many looked at land, got in touch, asked to book and bought.
- **Properties:** click a column to sort, for example by enquiries; click a row to open the property. *Interest* is enquiries plus visit requests for every 100 people who looked at it. Land with many views but little interest may need better photos or a price check.
- **Places**, **land types**, **where visits came from** and **devices.**

**Export CSV** downloads the numbers per property or per day for Excel. Visitors are counted once a day without cookies, so someone who comes back tomorrow counts again. Bots, and people whose browser asks not to be tracked, aren't counted.

## Notifications (the bell)

The bell at the top counts what's new for you: website enquiries, site visit and booking requests, payments recorded by colleagues, and new customer accounts. Click it to see the list; clicking an item opens it and marks it read. **Mark all as read** clears the count. You don't get a notification for things you did yourself. Notifications older than 90 days are removed.

Customers with an account also get updates in **My account** — including when land they saved changes price, gets reserved or sold, so keep prices and availability up to date.

## Customer accounts

Customers can (optionally) sign in on the website with a code sent to their email. They can save land and see their own enquiries and site visits in **My account**, in plain words (e.g. "We're in touch", "Confirmed") — they never see your notes or who is assigned.

Requests from a customer with an account show **Has an account** at the top. Anything they asked as a guest with the same email joins their account when they first sign in. If a customer asks to delete their account they can do it themselves in My account; their past requests stay with you.

## When customers compare land

Customers can tap **Compare** on up to 3 properties and see them side by side, including the **price per sq ft** (so plots in cents, acres and sq yards compare fairly). They often share that page on WhatsApp with family. Keep **road width, facing, water, electricity, approval numbers and amenities** filled in for every property — empty boxes show as "—" right next to a competitor's full details.

## Super admins only

- **Admin users**: invite people (Admin or Super Admin), change roles, deactivate.
- **Settings** (Super Admin): **Brand and company**: the tagline (footer and link previews), the line under the logo, the legal company name, and optionally the registered office address, **GST number** and **RERA number**. The legal name shows in the footer, on Contact and on the Terms, Privacy and Disclaimer pages; the address and numbers show wherever they are filled in. Then: company phone, WhatsApp, email, address, office hours, and the **sales notification emails** that receive new enquiries and visit requests. At the bottom, **Site visits**: which days and times customers can book, holidays, and how many days ahead (save with **Save site visit settings**).
- **Locations & lists**: cities, property types and amenities shown in the website filters.

## Something wrong?

- The panel shows a red message explaining what went wrong; read it first (e.g. "Add at least one photo before publishing").
- Website doesn't show a change after a minute? Refresh the page; if still wrong, tell the developer with the property code.
- Contacts are in [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md) § 5.
